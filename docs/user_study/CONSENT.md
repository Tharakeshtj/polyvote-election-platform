# Participant information and consent

**Study:** how easy is PolyVote to use? (BACSE202 Database Systems project, Vellore Institute of Technology)
**Team:** Tharakesharasu Thennarasu, M Pavan Durga Karthik, Shivam Malge. **Guide:** Dr. Katari Balakrishna.

**What you will do.** Use a demo election website for about 25 minutes: cast a vote (or set up and close an election), then answer a short questionnaire. We watch and take notes. You are testing the system, not being tested.

**What we record.** Whether you completed each task, how long it took, any errors, and your questionnaire answers. We do not record your name, your real password or your real email address. The demo shows a one-time code on screen, so you do not need to use a real mailbox. If we record the screen, it is only with your separate permission below.

**Risks and benefits.** No known risk beyond ordinary computer use. You may get tired or find a task frustrating; you can stop at any time without giving a reason and without any consequence. There is no payment.

**What happens to the data.** Your answers are stored under a participant number such as P07 and used in a project report and possibly a research paper. Individual answers will not be linked to you.

**Questions.** Ask the moderator now, or later through the team.
