## 3. Legitimate vs predatory checklist
- The conference or journal has a named organiser, committee and program committee with checkable affiliations, not just a generic email or gmail address.
- Publisher is named on the venue's own page and the volume appears on that publisher's site (SpringerLink, IEEE Xplore, ACM DL) for past editions. "Recommended to IEEE" or "submitted for indexing" is a plan, not a result.
- Check an IEEE conference number or the conference record in Xplore, and look up Springer volumes by ISBN/DOI.
- Real peer review with a stated timeline of weeks to months; very short (days) acceptance, or "acceptance in 24 hours", is a warning.
- Fees are stated plainly and are normal for the region; no fee for a "guaranteed" publication, no pushing extra indexing or "special issue" add-ons.
- Scope fits the paper; very broad "all engineering" scopes and conference names that closely imitate famous ones are warnings.
- Domain, editions and numbering are consistent year to year (ICSCAN and ICCCNT pages had conflicting edition text).
- Scopus/WoS claims should be confirmed in the Scopus source list or Web of Science master journal list, not on the venue's own banner.

## 4. Recommended plan

Dual submission is not allowed, so submit one venue at a time and wait for the decision.

| Rank | Role | Venue | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary | ICISSP 2027, position/regular paper, 29 Oct 2026 | Only strong-fit open window this week; accepts practical work; notification 4 Dec. Costs: registration plus travel to Malta; Scopus only "submitted for evaluation". |
| 2 | Backup | INDIACom-2027, 7 Dec 2026 | Submit if ICISSP rejects on 4 Dec; India-based, information-security track; IEEE Xplore not yet confirmed so VIT credit uncertain. |
| 3 | Backup | E-Vote-ID 2027, Track 3 or short paper/Track 4 demo (CFP not out yet) | Best topical audience; likely mid-2027 deadlines (unverified). Watch e-vote-id.org. |
| 4 | Stretch | DASFAA 2027 Demonstration track, 10 Dec 2026 | Database system demo and a Springer-LNCS-style venue, but competitive, needs a polished demo, and China travel. |
| 5 | Stretch | ICISS 2027 Industry/Demo track or Voting'27 short paper (CFPs not out) | ICISS names e-voting; Voting'27 takes 8-page short papers. Both need a stronger security analysis and wait for the CFP. |

Order: ICISSP (29 Oct) -> if rejected, INDIACom (7 Dec) or DASFAA demo (10 Dec) -> 2027 cycle (i-PACT 31 May 2027 for an on-campus IEEE option, E-Vote-ID, ICISS, CODS) -> journal extension (PeerJ CS or JISA) once there is new material.

If you cannot meet 29 Oct, skip straight to INDIACom and plan the 2027 cycle.

Notes: venues require original, author-written work and each has its own policy on disclosing AI assistance in writing or coding (ICISS, for example, asks authors to disclose generative-AI use at submission). Read each venue's policy and follow it. Any paper must not be under review elsewhere, and an extended journal version usually needs substantial new content and disclosure of the earlier version.
