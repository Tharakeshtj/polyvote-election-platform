## 5. Recommended submission order

**Dual submission is not allowed** at any of these venues (Confluence, DASFAA, ICDF2C, ICEIS and ICISSP state it explicitly; the others were not checked for that sentence, so assume the same). Submit to one venue at a time and wait for the decision before using the next one. Because decisions arrive between 4 Dec and mid-Feb, you realistically get two or three attempts before the spring 2027 deadlines. Also remember double-blind rules (ICISSP and ICEIS: remove names and do not post the paper on preprint servers while under review; DATA and SECRYPT use the same INSTICC rules but I did not read that page separately) and AI-use disclosure (ICISSP and ICEIS: in the acknowledgements; IEEE venues follow IEEE guidance).

| Step | Venue | Submit by | Decision | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ICISSP 2027, position or regular paper | 29 Oct 2026 | 4 Dec 2026 | Best fit that is open now (security of information systems, practical work accepted, 8-page short format). Costs EUR 640 plus Malta travel; indexing only 'submitted'. |
| 2a | INDIACom-2027 (cheap, India) | 7 Dec 2026 | 8 Feb 2027 | If ICISSP rejects on 4 Dec. INR 8,000 (+GST), 6 pages, security track; IEEE Xplore not confirmed, so VIT credit is uncertain. |
| 2b | Confluence 2027 (cheap, India, fast) | 4 Dec 2026 | 25 Dec 2026 | Alternative to 2a if you want a fast answer: same INR 8,000 (+GST), IEEE Xplore 'submitted for'. The deadline is the same day as the ICISSP decision, so the paper must already be formatted for 5-6 pages. Pick 2a or 2b, not both. |
| 3 | ICEIS 2027 stage 2 or IFIP SEC 2027 or ICDF2C 2027 | 21 Dec / 15 Dec / 15 Dec 2026 | 15 Feb / 18 Feb 2027 | Better-quality fallbacks (ICEIS names database security; IFIP SEC is Springer AICT with a 14-page limit; ICDF2C takes 6-11 page short papers). Only if step 1 failed and you did not already commit to 2a (its decision is 8 Feb). |
| 4 | ISDFS 2027 (online possible) | 31 Jan 2027 | 15 Feb 2027 | IEEE-submitted 6-page paper, USD 500 student rate, can be presented online. |
| 5 | ICEDEG 2027 (e-democracy) or DATA/SECRYPT 2027 | 28 Feb / 16 Feb-25 Mar 2027 | Apr / Apr-May 2027 | Best topical audience (ICEDEG) and strongest database-integrity match (DATA); fees and travel are the highest of the list. |
| 6 | IEEE CONECCT 2027 (Bangalore, call opens Dec 2026, deadline 15 Dec) or i-PACT 2027 (31 May 2027, on your campus) | Dec 2026 / May 2027 | Feb / Jun 2027 | Only if earlier attempts fail; CONECCT is not open yet; i-PACT is the on-campus, IEEE-submitted, INR 12,000 option. |

If you cannot meet 29 Oct, skip step 1 and start with 2a/2b. If low cost and an IEEE label are the priority, the cheapest IEEE-linked India options are Confluence, ICRCICN (30 Nov, INR 8,500, IEEE record #73691), ICESICSC (30 Oct, INR 9,000), and INDIACom. Do not use AJCC, and treat CSP, ICCDM, ICDAM and ICICC as lower-confidence choices (see their red flags). Avoid Privacy Symposium (data-protection law audience; closes 25 Oct) and the top-tier crypto/data venues (ACNS, ICDE, PerCom) unless you add substantial new content.

### Other caveats

- Several Indian conferences state deadlines that have already been extended once or twice (iConCISE, ICCIDS, AIS2C); another extension is possible but not documented.
- Fees are per accepted paper and are normally due after acceptance, not at submission, except where stated. Fees are non-refundable at most of these venues.
- ICICC, ICDAM and ICCDM share one organiser family and use 'proposed' or 'approved' Springer LNNS language; check the actual SpringerLink volume for the previous edition before paying.
- Exchange rates and fees can change; recheck on the venue page before paying.
