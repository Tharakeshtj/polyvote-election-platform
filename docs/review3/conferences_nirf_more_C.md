## 0. How this was done, and its limits (read first)

- **NIRF edition: India Rankings 2025** (Overall, Engineering, University; 100 rows each). `WebFetch` gets HTTP 403 from nirfindia.org, but `curl` with a browser user-agent returned all three tables (HTTP 200) and I parsed them locally. Ranks below are 2025 ranks (O = Overall, E = Engineering, U = University). I did not re-check whether a 2026 list is out.
- **Search tool limits.** `WebSearch` is US-only and snippet-based, and its shared budget ran out partway through (about 45 searches used; the tool then refused further calls). I did not work around that. After that I used only direct fetches of official pages: institution event pages, the official WordPress REST feeds of bits-pilani.ac.in and amrita.edu, the KIIT calendar, LPU's conference index, SRM's events index, IEEE vTools (events.vtools.ieee.org) search, and conference microsites. Roughly 150 page fetches in total. Several university sites render their event lists with JavaScript (UPES, MAHE, Thapar, GITAM, Christ, KLU, Saveetha) and could not be enumerated; "nothing found" for those means "nothing visible to a plain fetch", not "nothing exists".
- "IEEE record number visible" means a number printed on the official page I read (or, where stated, an IEEE vTools announcement). It is never a guarantee of Xplore inclusion. Everything else is the organiser's claim.
- Fees are the student-author fee in INR as printed, usually before 18% GST unless stated.

Ranks used (NIRF 2025): SRM IST O 22 / E 14 / U 11. LPU O 49 / E 48 / U 31. Chandigarh University O 32 / E 31 / U 19. Amity O 37 / E 37 / U 22. BITS Pilani O 16 / E 11 / U 7. ## 4. Details and fee-waiver / reimbursement information

None of these pages publishes a student fee waiver, scholarship or reimbursement. Specifics:

- **ICRITO 2027**: 6 pages, IEEE format; the IEEE-member discount is 20% on the computed fee; late registration adds INR 1,000; no-shows are removed from Xplore. Fee amounts are not shown on the pages I could read.
- **NGCAPS-2027**: double-blind, at least 2 reviews, target acceptance 40%, about 200 submissions expected; fees listed above; no refund policy read.
- **ICDSBS 2027 / CoaCoNS 2027**: Microsoft CMT submission; CoaCoNS says "Presented papers only will be considered for publication" and hybrid presentation is only for international authors.
- **ICFCIT 2027**: "registration fee ... will not be fully refunded"; no refund once the paper is scheduled; payment is by NEFT/UPI, with the payer bearing bank charges.
- **WcCST-2027**: at least one author must register and present; extra pages INR 1,000 each beyond 6.
- **ASCML 2027**: 50% refund if cancelled by 21 Dec 2026; best-presentation prize; no UG fee category.
- **RFCoN 2027**: single-blind, 6 pages including references, plagiarism and AI-text checks.
- **GTSS 2027**: 6 pages IEEE double-column, double-blind, all dates "tentative".

## 5. Recommendation for this paper (5-8 page IEEE paper, undergraduates)

Best new fits: **ICRITO 2027** (Amity; printed record number 72560; full paper 15 Dec 2026; 6 pages; explicit UG/PG category) and **NGCAPS-2027** (LPU; security, cryptography, data-mining/database and software-engineering tracks; deadline 15 Nov 2026; INR 8,000-9,000; IEEE Xplore only "proposed", no record number), then **GTSS 2027** (MMMUT, outside this batch; record number only on the vTools notice; deadline 31 Oct 2026). **ICDSBS 2027** (SRM, deadline 5 Jan 2027, INR 7,000-8,000) is the best late option; resolve the odd record number first. WcCST-2027's IEEE co-sponsorship is "subject to approval". Page limits: NGCAPS, ICRITO, GTSS, WcCST are 6 pages; ICDSBS and CoaCoNS are 4-5 pages, which would force cuts to a 5-8 page draft.
