## 0. Honest limits of this search

- The search tool is US-only and returns thin snippets; it indexes the IIT sites and IEEE calendars very poorly. Most queries of the form "<institution> conference 2027" returned the same handful of non-IIT pages (JIIT ICSC 2027, Vishwakarma ICMDRSE, IATMSI, RAIT, ICMC). "Nothing found" below means "nothing the tool surfaced and I could read", not "nothing exists".
- The IEEE conference calendar (conferences.ieee.org) returned HTTP 418, vTools search was not fetchable, WikiCFP refused the connection, and the aggregators conferencealerts.co.in, conferenceindex.org, allconferencealert.net and internationalconferencealerts.com returned 403. I could therefore NOT sweep the IEEE calendar for India events in Dec 2026 - Sep 2027. Only vTools pages that appeared as search hits were read.
- The web-search budget ran out (200 per turn, shared) before I finished; the last two queries (ICITSIF 2026 edition pattern) were refused. About 110 searches and about 70 fetches were made before that.
- nirfindia.org returned HTTP 403 to my fetches this session (it was readable for the previous researcher on 23 Oct). NIRF 2025 ranks below therefore come from a third-party mirror, https://corpready.in/nirf/2025/Engineering and https://corpready.in/nirf/2025/Overall (titled "NIRF India Rankings 2025"), and, for IIT Bhubaneswar, from the institute's own press release (https://www.iitbbs.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Press-Release-NIRF-India-Ranking-2025-IIT-Bhubaneswar-jumps-15-spots-to-39th-rank-in-the-Engineering-category.pdf: Engineering 39, Overall 80). The mirror agrees with the previous researcher on every rank they quoted (IISc O2, IIT Kharagpur O6/E5, IIT Dhanbad O35/E15, IIT Jammu E56, IIT Palakkad E64, IIIT Bangalore E69, ABV-IIITM E96, SSN O82/E47).
